How to Use This Guide

This guide turns the 21 chapters of Understanding AI in Pharma (v11) into question banks for 30-minute conversations with pharma CXOs, VPs and Directors. It is deliberately longer than any single conversation can use. You choose; the guide helps you choose well.

Your hat. You speak as a consultancy practitioner, not as a model-firm voice. Say so up front, ask what leaders need from outside partners, and never ask them to rank you against model firms or other consultancies.

Four rules

01

Claim, then question

Put one sharp claim on the table, then ask. A blank question makes the leader do all the work. A claim lets them agree, correct you or add nuance, which is how you show judgment.

02

Frameworks over figures

Most figures in the book are secondary or vendor-sourced (Appendix C). Use the durable frameworks instead: the five tests for an AI announcement, the three tests for disease areas, the context-of-use register, and written stop criteria.

03

Evidence grade sets your tone

Chapters graded 5/10 or higher can be stated. Grades of 3–4 should be hedged. Grades of 2 or lower should only be asked, never asserted. The priority map shows each chapter’s grade.

04

Leave something behind

End every conversation with a concrete next step: a one-line takeaway, an intro, and an offer to share an anonymized synthesis after 5–10 conversations. That synthesis earns the second meeting.

Selection rule

Each chapter bank has an opener list, a core list and a closer list. Pick one opener, two core questions from the core list, and one closer, and keep one more core question in reserve. Match the function using the chapter banks and the level using this modifier: CXO means bets, capital and stop criteria; VP means ownership, scale-out and accountability; Director means validation, workflows and the last real blocker.

本指南怎么用

本指南把《Understanding AI in Pharma》（v11）的 21 章转化为面向制药企业 CXO、VP、总监的 30 分钟对话问题库。它刻意比任何一次对话能用的更长：由您来选，本指南帮您选得更好。

您的身份。 您以咨询公司从业者的身份发言，而不是模型公司的声音。开场就说明这一点，询问领导者需要外部伙伴做什么，绝不要求对方把您与模型公司或其他咨询公司排名。

四条规则

01

先主张，再提问

先抛出一个尖锐的主张，再提问。空白提问让对方做全部的功；主张让对方可以同意、纠正或补充，这正是展示判断力的方式。

02

框架优先于数字

书中多数数字来自二手来源或厂商（见附录 C）。请改用经得起时间的框架：评估 AI 公告的五个问题、判断疾病领域的三个检验、使用情境登记册，以及书面写下的停止标准。

03

证据评级决定语气

证据评级 5/10 及以上的章节可以陈述；3–4 要加限定；2 及以下只能提问，不可断言。优先级地图列出了每章的评级。

04

留下点什么

每次对话以一个具体的下一步收尾：一句话要点、一次引荐，以及在 5–10 次对话后分享匿名化综合的邀约。这份综合会为您赢得第二次见面。

30 分钟流程

时间内容
0–3 分钟开场：说明您为什么来问、一句话的观点，以及您会带回什么。
3–8 分钟破题：从章节的破题列表中选一个问题（例如 1.1, 3.1, 4.1、U.1 或 U.2）。
